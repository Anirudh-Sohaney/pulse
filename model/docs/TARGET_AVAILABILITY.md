# Target Availability Audit

## Review note — 2026-09-14

Target availability remains the limiting factor for strong claims. The
expanded input inventory provides many possible predictors, but a predictor is
not a target and a public proxy is not local pharmacy inventory. Promotion
requires a chronological held-out target with enough observations, a simple
baseline, and the declared accuracy threshold. Current public outputs remain
Arkansas-first proxies, with national, neighboring-state, or global context
kept at its native geography when no defensible local mapping exists.

This audit separates an observed pharmacy target from an operational input or
an external proxy. A target is not promoted merely because its source is
downloadable; its cadence, geography, population, and supplier resolution must
match the forecast claim.

## Rejected direct near-term sources

- Arkansas PDMP reports controlled prescriptions from Arkansas pharmacies on
  the next business day, but access is limited to authorized users and it only
  covers controlled substances. It is not a freely available training source.
  See the [Arkansas PDMP description](https://healthy.arkansas.gov/programs-services/prevention-healthy-living/prescription-drug-monitoring-program/).
- CDC opioid, buprenorphine, and naloxone dispensing maps are useful public
  validation context, but the published series is annual and based on IQVIA
  Xponent rather than an open weekly pharmacy feed. See the [CDC dispensing
  maps](https://www.cdc.gov/overdose-prevention/data-research/facts-stats/us-dispensing-rate-maps.html).
- CMS also publishes a free Medicare Part D opioid-prescribing geography file,
  but the currently accessible file is a single 2024 annual snapshot. It is
  not a chronological training panel and therefore is not promoted as a
  forecast metric. See the [CMS geographic opioid file](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/medicare-part-d-opioid-prescribing-rates-by-geography).
- Public CMS SDUD and Part D files do not expose weekly pharmacy inventory,
  pharmacy fill-level events, wholesaler allocation, or county-by-supplier
  fulfillment. Those labels must remain unclaimed until a permissible source
  is obtained.

## Promotion rule

The county region labels use the published Arkansas DHS/TEFRA five-region
county definition, not a geographic clustering inferred from demand. See the
[Arkansas TEFRA regional analysis](https://humanservices.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/TEFRAAttachI.pdf).

Historical three-state artifacts remain compatibility research outputs under
the revised goal; newly promoted rows must be numeric or five-state. The
publishable suite therefore exposes quarterly statewide demand/shortage,
annual county demand, weekly national acquisition cost, monthly national
supplier shortage continuation, monthly five-state NDC shortage-pressure
states, weekly national respiratory pressure, and weekly Arkansas NSSP
influenza pressure. Weekly or monthly
county-by-supplier pharmacy demand and inventory are explicitly unavailable,
not imputed from ARCOS, weather, news, or supplier shortage records.
